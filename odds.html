Extras

Cover in 11th edition does not improve a save — it makes you harder to hit, one fixed −1, however many ways you qualify for it. Rapid Fire and Blast both add to the Attacks stepper above them once switched on. Torrent ignores the hit roll entirely, including cover, re-rolls and Sustained/Lethal Hits.

This is arithmetic, not the rules. It works out averages from the numbers you type in. Where it disagrees with the official rules, the official rules win — Games Workshop publishes the 11th edition Core Rules as a free PDF and in the Warhammer 40,000 app.

It assumes one model, one weapon profile. The toggles above cover Sustained Hits, Lethal Hits, Devastating Wounds, Feel No Pain, Torrent, Heavy, Anti-X, Lance, Rapid Fire, Blast, minimum damage, Ignores Cover and the common re-roll abilities — but not Extra Attacks (a second weapon profile fired alongside the first) or Precision (targeting a specific model in the unit), since both need more than one profile or one target to mean anything. Damage that spills past the model's last wound is wasted, which is how a single-model target works — against a full unit, extra damage does not carry to the next model either.

Averages are not promises. A 31% chance to kill something means it survives most of the time.
