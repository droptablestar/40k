Combat Patrol: Tyranid Assault Brood

  • Parasite of Mortrex M12″ T5 Sv4+ W5 Ld6+ OC1

    Infantry, Character, Fly. Your Warlord and only Combat Patrol Character — flies, deep strikes, and can't be targeted while a closer non-Character unit is around (Lone Operative).

    Barbed ovipositor — Melee A2 WS2+ S3 AP-2 D3, hits Infantry harder and gets extra swings.
    Clawed limbs — Melee A6 WS2+ S5 AP-1 D1.

    It Itches! — at the start of the Fight phase, force one enemy unit within range to take a battle-shock test.

  • Genestealers (x10) M8″ T4 Sv5+ W2 Ld7+ OC1

    Infantry. 5+ invulnerable save. Can be split into two 5-model units before the battle starts (Patrol Squads) and Scouts 8" ahead at deployment.

    Genestealer claws and talons — Melee A4 WS2+ S4 AP-2 D1.

    Vanguard Predator — re-roll a Hit roll of 1; also re-roll a Wound roll of 1 when attacking near an objective marker.

  • Biovore M5″ T6 Sv3+ W5 Ld8+ OC1

    Infantry. Explodes on death and can hurt nearby units (Deadly Demise 1) — don't stand your other models too close when it dies.

    Spore Mine launcher — Ranged, 48″ A D3 BS4+ S6 AP-1 D2, indirect blast fire that ignores some invulnerable saves (Devastating Wounds).
    Chitin-barbed limbs — Melee A2 WS4+ S5 AP0 D1.

  • Tyrant Guard (x3) M6″ T8 Sv3+ W4 Ld8+ OC1

    Infantry. Your durable synapse bodyguards — high toughness and wounds compared to the rest of the box. Each of the three comes with a different melee loadout, fixed per model:

    Bone cleaver, lash whip and rending claws — Melee A3 WS3+ S5 AP-1 D2.
    Crushing claws and rending claws — Melee A2 WS4+ S8 AP-2 D2, twin-linked (re-roll wound rolls).
    Scything talons and rending claws — Melee A5 WS3+ S5 AP-1 D1.
