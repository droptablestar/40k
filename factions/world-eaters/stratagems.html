Berzerker Warband — Stratagems

  • Blood Offering 1 CP · Any phase
    If a World Eaters unit holding an objective is destroyed, you can keep control of that objective until your opponent's control there exceeds yours.
  • Hack and Slash 1 CP · Fight phase
    Give a unit that charged this turn and hasn't fought yet +1 to the Armour Penetration of its melee weapons for the phase.
  • Frenzied Resilience 1 CP · Enemy shoot/fight
    Right after an enemy declares targets against one of your units, subtract 1 from the Damage of attacks allocated to it for the rest of the phase.
  • Skulls for the Skull Throne! CP unclear · Fight phase
    Right after a unit destroys a Character or Monster in the Fight phase, make a Blessings of Khorne roll and activate an extra Blessing for the rest of the round.
  • Apoplectic Frenzy CP unclear · Movement phase
    Right after a Khorne Berzerkers unit Advances, it can still declare a charge this turn despite having Advanced.
  • Berzerker's Wrath CP unclear · Enemy shooting
    When a Khorne Berzerkers unit makes a Blood Surge move after being shot, it moves a flat 8" instead of rolling a D6.

Berzerker Warband — Enhancements

  • Berzerker Glaive World Eaters only
    +1 to the Attacks and Damage of the bearer's melee weapons (not weapons with Extra Attacks). Pure offense.
  • Helm of Brazen Ire World Eaters only
    -1 Damage from any attack allocated to the bearer. The defensive pick.
  • Favoured of Khorne World Eaters only
    Re-roll up to two dice on your Blessings of Khorne roll while the bearer is on the battlefield — makes the army-wide buff roll more reliable.
  • Battle-lust World Eaters only
    Re-roll Charge rolls for the bearer's unit; if Unbridled Bloodlust is active that round, add 1 to those Charge rolls too.
