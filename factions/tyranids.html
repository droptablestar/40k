Combat Patrol: Tyranid Assault Brood

Scoped to what we're actually playing — the Assault Brood Combat Patrol box, not the full Tyranids codex. Combat Patrol is self-contained by design: a fixed 15-model roster, no detachment to choose, and its own small rules card rather than a full codex — sourced here from the box's actual rules-card PDF, not guessed from wiki summaries.

What's in the box, and how it plays

Not the full rules — just what this box feels like to face and to field.

Synapse

While a Tyranids unit is within 6" of a friendly synapse model, it tests battle-shock on 3D6 instead of 2D6. Parasite of Mortrex and Tyrant Guard are your synapse models in this box — keep them within 6" of the Genestealers and Biovores so a bad battle-shock roll doesn't strip models off the board.

Shadow in the Warp

Once per battle, in either player's Command phase, you can force every enemy unit on the table to take a battle-shock test. One-shot, so timing it against a unit that's already taken losses gets the most out of it.

Fixed 15-model roster, no detachment choice

Parasite of Mortrex (your Warlord), 10 Genestealers, 3 Tyrant Guard, 1 Biovore. Combat Patrol doesn't ask you to pick a detachment — this is the whole force, and it comes with its own 3 stratagems and 2 secondary objectives, distinct from full-game ones.

Genestealers do the killing

Ten Genestealers are the melee threat — fast (8" move, Scouts 8") and hit hard, but only a 5+ invulnerable save between them and anything shooting back. Tyrant Guard and the Biovore exist to protect them or soften a target before they arrive.
