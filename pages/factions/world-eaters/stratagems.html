Berzerker Warband — Enhancements

  • Berzerker Glaive World Eaters only
    +1 to the Attacks and Damage of the bearer's melee weapons (not weapons with Extra Attacks). Pure offense.
  • Helm of Brazen Ire World Eaters only
    -1 Damage from any attack allocated to the bearer. The defensive pick.
  • Favoured of Khorne World Eaters only
    Re-roll up to two dice on your Blessings of Khorne roll while the bearer is on the battlefield — makes the army-wide buff roll more reliable.
  • Battle-lust World Eaters only
    Re-roll Charge rolls for the bearer's unit; if Unbridled Bloodlust is active that round, add 1 to those Charge rolls too.
