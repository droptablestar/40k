Combat Patrol: Bane-Slayer's Bulwark

Scoped to what we're actually playing — the Bane-Slayer's Bulwark Combat Patrol box, not the full League of Votann codex. Combat Patrol is self-contained by design: a fixed 19-model roster, no detachment to choose, and its own army-wide rule rather than a full codex.

What's in the box, and how it plays

Not the full rules — just what this box feels like to face and to field.

Prioritised Efficiency

Every League of Votann unit switches between two abilities depending on how many Yield Points (YP) you're holding: fewer than 7 and it has Hostile Acquisition, 7 or more and it has Fortify Takeover. The switch happens at the end of every Command phase, so the army's whole posture flips over the course of a game as YP builds up or gets spent.

Hostile Acquisition

The behind/early-game state. Attacks against an enemy unit near any objective marker get +1 to hit, and you can re-roll Advance and Charge rolls — this army wants to be moving up and grabbing ground while it's short on YP.

Fortify Takeover

The ahead/holding state, once YP hits 7. Attacks against any enemy get +1 to hit as long as you're near an objective you control, and units getting shot or fought by something stronger than them get harder to wound — this army turns defensive once it's sitting on points.

Yield Points

The currency that drives the switch above. You gain 1 YP each Command phase for holding an objective in your own deployment zone, plus more from the second round onward for holding objectives outside it and for controlling more objectives than your opponent. Spending YP on other rules (elsewhere in the full codex) reduces the total back down, which can flip a unit back to Hostile Acquisition.
