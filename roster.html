    The stages

    Same order of work as your first model, end to end, with the three prep steps — clipping, mould lines, glue — counted as one.

      Table-ready is the base coat through to the base rim. A model counts once it is painted, shaded, brought back up, detailed and based — the point where it reads clearly from arm's length, which is what table-ready means here. Varnish protects the paint rather than changing how it looks, so it is the stage after the line, not part of it.

      The percentage is weighted by miniatures, not by units — ten finished infantry move it further than one finished character.

      This log lives in this browser on this device only. It does not sync to your other phone, and clearing site data clears it.
